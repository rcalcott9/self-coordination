# Roundabout (the un-hackable design)

`2026-07-19` · roundabout_solution

## What it tested

Private per-seat rotation+mirror; convention exit = 10, two symmetric 15-point exits.

## What it found

0/10 leaks - un-hackable by construction. The group holds the convention because the two 15-exits cannot be coordinated a priori.
