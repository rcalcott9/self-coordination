# Confidence calibration (3 arms)

`2026-08-01` · confidence_calibration

## What it tested

Arm 1 choice -> true match rate; arm 2 stated P; arm 3 safe-payoff sweep -> revealed confidence. Both models, 6-stimulus graded battery.

## What it found

Neither model is calibrated, and it fails in both directions - underconfident where coordination is perfect, overconfident on pure noise. The safe range {3,5,7,9} fails to bracket the switch point on most stimuli, so 10 of 12 revealed-confidence points are censored.
