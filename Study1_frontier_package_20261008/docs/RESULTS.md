## Study 1: frontier minimal collection

**Integrity.**
- 2,680 calls (preflight 8, pilot 48, anchor 328, main 2,296): every call parsed, 0 model mismatches, $23.60.
- Calibration drift test (pre-specified gate) did not detect drift: p = .95 for GPT, .94 for Opus. The test was conservative, with high power against broad randomisation and favourite changes and almost none against concentration-only change.
- Frame calibration was reused under the frozen rule.

### Post hoc exploratory: cross-model divergence

- **Favourite object:** the two models share it on 31 of 41 arrays.
- **Matching probability:** r = .55. Mean |Δy| = .19, against a noise floor of .05.
- **Divergence vs error:** after controlling for the focal model's matching level, neither auditor error nor self error tracked divergence (all CIs include 0).
- **Baseline:** neither the auditors nor the self-predictions beat a simple baseline that uses the predicting model's own measured y. Auditor predictions loaded more on the auditor's own model's y (.26 / .19) than on the focal model's y (.15 / .15).

## Study 1b: crossed object-level prediction

**Integrity.**
- 5,334 calls (screen 2,736, preflight 6, pilot 32, main 2,560): every call parsed, 0 mismatches, $39.34.
- Both gates passed.
- Selected arrays: S1 5 (3 via the pre-registered ≥ .70 fallback), S2_G 3, S2_O 2, so the primary sample is n = 10 arrays from **7 independent families**. S3 has 10 arrays.

- hierarchical bootstrap CI [.111, .421];

### Secondary results

- **S3 shared-policy controls:** pooled +.000 [−.014, +.013].
- **Continuous moderation by screening total variation distance:** slope +.485 [.241, .715], permutation p < .001. This is descriptive and largely restates the S3 vs S1 contrast built into the design.

### Post hoc exploratory: projection diagnostic

- Each predictor gave nearly the same distribution to both targets (mean total variation distance between its two predictions: .067 for GPT, .048 for Opus).
- Cross-predictions sat closer to the predictor's own policy (GPT 10 of 10 arrays, Opus 6 of 10).
- Predicted target differences did not align with measured ones (cosine −.26 for GPT, +.12 for Opus).

## Interpretation: two claims kept apart

| Claim | Status |
|---|---|
| **Predictive self-advantage:** each model's predictions about itself are more accurate than the other model's, on arrays where their policies differ | **Demonstrated** (Study 1b primary; holds per target, survives family aggregation, absent on shared-policy controls) |
| **Relational self-knowledge:** a model represents that its policy differs from the other's | **Not demonstrated.** The diagnostic is compatible with self-projection, but the design cannot separate projection from relational knowledge |
| **Matching-probability self-advantage over a reciprocal auditor** (Study 1 P1) | Not detected for GPT; borderline and not established for Opus |
